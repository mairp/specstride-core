# B-8.4c — Rulings review: D1 to D3, K1 to K9, P1 to P16 and the approvals without a question

Reviewer: independent agent. Date: 2026-09-29.

Scope: every section of `docs/ecosystem/APPROVAL.md` after the numbered rulings 1 to 70: D1 to D3,
K1 to K9 (with the defect policy of `consolidation.md` section 5 and the superseded and dropped
requirements of `traceability.md` section 4.3), "Approved without a separate question", "Not approved,
because not asked", and P1 to P16. Rulings 1 to 70 were read for context only.

Method, in short:

- `grep -rnE` over `specs/1*/spec.md` and `specs/100-specstride/annexes/*.md` for each ruling id and for
  its subject (key names, literals, integrity kinds, flag names).
- Two small scripts in the scratch directory `B-8.4c/`: `dv.sh` lists every requirement whose
  `divergence:` line cites a given register row; `bi.sh` lists every requirement whose `origin:` line
  cites a given behaviour item, with its disposition, test and parity lines.
- Every carrying requirement named below was read in the file. Plans and task lists were checked where a
  ruling sets a number, a default or an order (`grep` in `plan.md` and `tasks.md` of the carrying feature).
- Baseline facts were read with `git -C /root/specstride show 9cab15d:<path>` and from
  `research/R18-behaviour-inventory.tsv`.
- Queued questions CQ-017 to CQ-181 were searched for each candidate finding; none of the findings below
  is a queued question.
