# B-8.4b — Rulings review: rulings 36 to 70

Reviewer: independent agent. Date: 2026-09-29.

Scope: the rulings 36 to 70 of `docs/ecosystem/APPROVAL.md`, section "Rulings on the 90 entries of
`DECISIONS-PENDING.md`", with the lettered sub-ruling 57a: 36 rulings. For each one: is it reflected in
at least one requirement of `specs/1*/spec.md` or of `specs/100-specstride/annexes/`, or is there a
stated reason why it needs none (prompt §6.4). Rulings 1 to 35, D1 to D3, K1 to K9 and P1 to P16 were
read for context only.

Method, in short:

1. `cite.py` (scratch): every line of the 41 `spec.md` files and the 9 annexes that names a ruling of
   the part by number ("ruling 43", "rulings 46, 47, 55", "owner ruling 52"). 263 lines. Result: the
   numbers stand almost only in the `Sources` line, in the assumption tables and in the clarification
   notes; only four requirements name a ruling of the part in their own text or in `origin:`.
2. `req.py` (scratch): parses the 1,892 requirement and success-criterion blocks (`- **F<n>-FR-<nnn>**`
   with their field lines) and searches them by subject. Each ruling was searched by subject, and the
   requirements found were read in full.
3. For the rulings that set a number, a default or an order: `grep` over `plan.md`, `tasks.md`,
   `data-model.md` and `contracts/` of the carrying feature, and over annex X §X.7 (the `parity` profile).
4. For the rulings of `HANDOFF.md` section 2: a second search for the overruled default in any artifact.
5. Every finding was checked against the file; places are `path:line`.

Verdicts over the 36 rulings: 33 `reflected`, 3 `partly` (37, 49, 57 with 57a), 0 `contradicted`,
0 `missing`, 0 `none needed`. Two rulings (62 and 63) are reflected in Wave 1 only in the part that

What holds: no spec carries an overruled default. Content capture follows traces and is off only under
`parity` (ruling 43); the critic with read-only tools is specified in the working tree with the
proposer's files hidden (57, 57a); parallel units and the model-tier bump are Wave 1 options that are
off by default (56, 48); Tier 3 ships refused (59); the runtime neither commits nor reverts inside a
phase attempt, apart from the two writes of P1 (50); the Agent SDK transport is Tier 2 with API keys
only and never bundled (70); a macOS runner is a release condition (67); no new line is printed under
`parity` (68); judgment may be delegated by a model-less host (64). The numbers of rulings 45, 46, 56,
59, 64 and 65 are the same in `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `tasks.md`, `data-model.md` and `contracts/`
wherever they occur, with the exceptions listed as findings 005 and 007.

The three most serious findings:

1. **B-8.4b-001** (medium): ruling 49 is carried for the choice of the critic (features 124, 125) and not
   for escalation, the subject of the question it answered (DP-052, R25 open question 4). As written, the
   escalation rule skips a rung on the critic's model whatever the effort, which is the outcome DP-052
   gave for the answer the owner did not choose.
2. **B-8.4b-002** (medium): ruling 37 has no requirement in the adapters 130 (dsh) and 138 (prime), both
   of which specify a critic. Feature 130 reports the memory switch as null, and feature 100 says that a
   harness that cannot switch its memory off does not qualify as critic.
3. **B-8.4b-003** (medium): F101-FR-020 says that the diagnostician holds no tools and also that it uses
   the critic of the role plan, which after rulings 57 and 57a may be of the form `read-only-tools`.
